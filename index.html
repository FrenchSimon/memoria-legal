Privacy policy

Updated November 11, 2026

In short

Mémoria has no account and no server. What you write about your people stays on your phone.

The only thing that can leave it is anonymous usage statistics, and only if you turn them on. They are off by default.

Who is responsible

Mémoria is developed by Simon Dabin, who is responsible for the processing described here. You can write to memoria.app.contact@gmail.com for any question about your data.

What stays on your phone

Mémoria keeps these in a database on your phone, to show them to you and help you review them:

  • your people: first name, last name, relationship, birthday;
  • your notes and the cards made from them;
  • your reviews: due dates and your answers (Forgot, Unsure, Knew it);
  • your settings: reminder, cards per session, language, app lock, statistics choice, and the dates of your first and latest opening.

A random identifier is created on your phone to link your reviews to you. It is never sent.

When you delete a note or a person, its content is erased right away, including from the database file. “Delete my data”, in Settings, erases everything. Uninstalling the app does too.

What you note about your people

You may note things about others, sometimes sensitive, like an allergy or a health worry. These notes stay on your phone, for your personal use. Mémoria never receives them, and they are never part of the statistics.

Your phone’s services

Mémoria relies on features of your phone, which handle some data under Apple’s or Google’s own privacy policies:

  • Dictation: your voice is turned into text by Apple’s or Google’s speech recognition, on the phone when it can, otherwise on their servers. Mémoria never receives the audio, only the text, which stays on your phone. Using dictation needs access to the microphone and to speech recognition.
  • Daily reminder: a local notification, scheduled on your phone, with a generic text that never shows a name or a note. Mémoria sends no remote notifications.
  • App lock: Face ID, Touch ID, your fingerprint or your phone’s passcode, checked by your phone. Mémoria only learns whether unlocking worked. With the lock on, Android also blocks screenshots of Mémoria.
  • Phone backups: your phone can include Mémoria’s data in its backups (Google on Android, iCloud or your computer on iPhone), depending on your phone’s settings. This lets you find your notes on a new phone. Mémoria has no access to these backups, and you can turn them off in your phone’s settings.

Anonymous statistics

They are optional, and off unless you turn them on, at the end of the welcome screens or in Settings.

What they contain: events like “note created”, “review finished” or “app opened”, with numbers and fixed values (how many cards, which category, kept or edited card, keyboard or dictation). Never what you write, a name, a birthday or anything that identifies a person or a note.

Technical data is added to each event: your phone’s system and version, its model, the app’s version, its language and a random session number. The server also sees your IP address: it only uses it to work out your country and region, and does not store it.

Why: to know whether Mémoria is useful (do people take notes, do they review) and improve it. Legal basis: your consent.

Who receives them: Aptabase, an analytics service that processes them on our behalf, hosted in Germany, in the European Union (with its providers AWS and Tinybird, also in the EU). They are kept for up to 5 years.

These statistics can’t be linked to you: there is no account, no advertising identifier and no device identifier. You can turn them off at any time in Settings. Events not yet sent are then dropped.

What Mémoria doesn’t do

  • no account, no advertising, no tracking across apps or websites;
  • no selling or sharing of your data;
  • no access to your location, your contacts or your photos;
  • no remote notifications.

Your rights

  • See and correct your data: everything is in the app.
  • Erase it: delete a note or a person, or use “Delete my data” in Settings.
  • Withdraw your consent to statistics: the switch in Settings.
  • Export your data: not available yet; it will come with accounts.

For any question or request, write to memoria.app.contact@gmail.com. You can also file a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (cnil.fr).
